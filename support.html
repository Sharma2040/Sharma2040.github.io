Common questions

Friends can't find my table

Keep the phones within a few metres with Bluetooth on (Wi-Fi on helps). Allow the Nearby devices permission, or Location on Android 12 and older. The host taps Host a table first; friends then tap Join a table. Let a friend in only if both screens show the same 4-digit code.

How do I change the language?

Settings (gear icon) → Language · भाषा → English, नेपाली or हिन्दी.

Can I buy chips or win money?

No. Chips and coins are for fun only. They can't be bought or exchanged for money.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Settings → Ads and privacy → Privacy choices (shown where required). You can also reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

How do I delete my data?

Taas Adda has no accounts. Everything (name, settings, coins, statistics, emergency contacts) is stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it all.

Does SOS call the police or an ambulance?

No. SOS alerts your friends at the table and can open a text to your emergency contacts. In danger, call emergency services yourself: Nepal 100 (police), 102 (ambulance); India 112; Korea 112 (police), 119 (fire and ambulance).
